# Mac Flow — three storylines

## A. "Look up" (built)

- **Device / form:** the feature as the hook + one shape that holds. The camera lives at the top of a MacBook screen; the notch wakes, and every beat is the island turning into the next thing a real afternoon needs. At the end the island folds back into the notch and the notch becomes the Mac Flow mark (the mark *is* the island: a stadium with a star cut through it).
- **First two seconds:** a macro of a MacBook's notch over a warm silk wallpaper; "Look up." lands under it in large type.
- **Beats:** hook (the notch does nothing) → wake (a song blooms out of it) → name (Mac Flow) → Join a meeting → ask Flow for focus + music, the island splits → a build races to done → AirPods → drop a file on the notch → montage of every presentation → Home opens as the camera pulls back to the whole Mac → "Everything live. Just look up." → the notch becomes the mark.
- **Look:** the product's own UI (rendered from the app's code) on an original warm silk desktop; SF Pro Display titles in ink; one indigo colour field for the build; paper-white end card with the brand lockup.
- **Sound:** 120 BPM minimal electronic score in F minor resolving to A♭ major: near silence and a ticking pulse under the hook, a drop on the wake, a breakdown under Flow, a hard 8th-note push under the montage, one riser into the final hit on the mark.

## B. "One afternoon, one Mac" (not built)

A moment in someone's day: the menu-bar clock is the carrier (10:24 → 18:30). Every cut advances the clock with a slot-machine roll; the light in the wallpaper moves from morning to evening while the island handles the day — meeting, focus, build, the commute home with AirPods. Form: one frame that rebuilds (the camera never leaves the notch). Sound: a warm piano-and-pulse bed that brightens through the day.

## C. "Doing nothing" (not built, the bold one)

Type carries the argument: giant kinetic SF Pro lines cut on the beat — "17 million notches." "Doing nothing." — and colour fields in each activity's own tint (orange timer, green call, blue download) flood the frame on every bar, with the black island hanging from the top edge of the frame as the only constant. Form: cut on the beat, match cuts on the stadium shape. Sound: a hard, percussive track with stops on every claim. (Would need a sourced figure for any number it states.)
