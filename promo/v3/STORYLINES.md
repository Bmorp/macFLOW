# Mac Flow v3 — storylines

What it is: Mac Flow 8 — the notch now runs your whole day (Flow plans it, sets the room, takes the meeting's notes,
understands what you copy, watches Claude, draws the day, and sings you out with StandBy).
The one thing to remember: "My Mac's notch runs my day."

All three: ~60 s, 16:9, 4K master + a 1080p cut for the README. Real app UI only (rendered from the app's own views).
Music and effects, no voiceover. Licensed (Pixabay) audio.

## A — "One day" (recommended: a stranger follows one day, and every latest feature is a moment in it)

Device: a moment in someone's day, the whole day. Form: object-to-object chain, cut on the beat.
7:58 AM to 11:40 PM on one Mac: the plan Flow drafted overnight, the room that sets itself, the meeting it writes up,
the copy it understands, the Claude session it watches, the day drawn as a ring, and StandBy singing at night. The clock
in the island ticks forward and the light changes with the app's own moods: peach morning, ember focus, magenta evening,
night.

- First 2 s: the island already open in morning peach light under the notch: "Plan your day", three priorities drafted
  ("Drafted from yesterday"); the cursor arrives on Start My Day.
- Beats: plan → Start My Day → Set the Room (apps return, the rest dims, focus ring, music) → "Design Review in 5 min"
  → Join → Take Notes → summary + Add to Tomorrow → copy "Lunch with Sam Fri 1pm" → Lens: Add to Calendar → Claude
  finished, Open → hold the notch: "What did I get done today?" → the Your Day ring draws itself (payoff) → 11:40 PM
  StandBy: the Liquid Glass clock tucked behind the dune, then Now Playing with floating lyrics → "Your day. In the
  notch." → end card.
- Look: v2's stage (black bezel, the 3D MacBook, island at 1.6–2× with depth) lit hour by hour in the app's mood
  colours; SF Pro; violet only for Flow.
- Sound: a bright produced track building through the morning, dropping at Join, breaking down for StandBy's lyrics,
  final hit on the mark; clicks on clicks, a whoosh per hour jump, one riser into the ring.

## B — "Faster than a click" (the bold one)

Device: type carries the argument. Form: hard cuts on every half beat.
Giant verbs slam in on the beat, each proved instantly by a real island punch-in in that feature's own colour:
Plan. Focus. Talk. Notes. Lens. Rewind. Learn. Sleep. Then the island fractures into a wall of forty live states that
collapses back into one pill, and the pill becomes the mark.

- First 2 s: the island mid-split (music + timer bubble) huge on violet; on beat one "Plan." smashes in and the planner
  opens under it.
- Beats: split → eight verb/proof pairs (~4 s each) → the wall of every state (payoff) → "One notch. Everything." → end card.
- Look: full-bleed colour fields from each feature's tint; SF Pro Display heavy at 22% of frame height, −4% tracking;
  UI flat at 2×, no 3D.
- Sound: a hard-hitting ~128 BPM track; a hit on every word, glitch whooshes on the cuts.

## C — "Into the notch"

Device: the object is the explanation. Form: one endless zoom, no cuts.
The camera starts on the MacBook at night and dives into the notch: Home → into the album cover, which becomes
StandBy's Now Playing → into a lyric line, which becomes Flow's star → into the star, the Day ring → into one arc, a
Rewind moment → … and the last zoom comes out of a grain of the dune wallpaper, back to the Mac asleep in StandBy.

- First 2 s: the MacBook in the dark, screen lit, already pushing in fast; the island blooms as the lens arrives.
- Beats: dive → Home → StandBy lyrics → Flow → Day ring → Rewind → Lens/Claude → pull-out to the Mac (payoff) → end card.
- Look: one continuous camera with depth of field; each world keeps its own ground (the rendered wallpapers, album
  colours, ink black).
- Sound: one building track; a riser through every dive, an impact at each landing, silence before the final pull-out.
